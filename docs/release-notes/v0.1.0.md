The first release of Holiday Photo Gallery: a free photo manager for Windows in the spirit of Windows Live Photo Gallery. Everything you add is saved in the photo itself, so it comes along when you copy or share your photos.

## Find and browse
- Add your photo folders and browse them by folder, by date, by place, or all at once.
- Albums for your trip folders, sorted by when each trip ended. Rename an album and its folder in one go.
- A filter bar for people, tags, camera, rating, flags, rejects, dates, uploads and details still to do.
- Ctrl+K finds any command, ? lists the keyboard shortcuts, and Ctrl+Z undoes the last change.

## Pick the best photos
- Compare 1 to 4 photos side by side, zoom and pan them together, and rate with the number keys.
- Flags (for example "to print") and a reject mark, saved in the file.
- Lightroom keyboard shortcuts, with W A S D as a second set of arrow keys.

## Dates, places and people
- Fix the date taken with its time zone, or shift a whole camera's photos at once.
- Place photos on a map (with a satellite view), estimate a spot from the photos before and after, and look up the place names.
- Faces are found and recognised on your PC, and names are saved so other photo apps see them too.
- Automatic tags from an AI model that runs on your PC, in your own words and language.

## Tidy up
- Import from a phone, camera or SD card, without copying anything twice.
- Videos and Live Photos in the library, with ratings, tags and places.
- Find duplicates, keep the best copy and give it the others' details.
- Rename a batch with a pattern (F2), copy and paste details between photos (Ctrl+Shift+C / Ctrl+Shift+V).
- Repair damaged or cut-off photos, and fix wrong file extensions.
- Private folders and photos stay hidden until you turn on private mode.

## Edit and share
- Crop, rotate, automatic brightness and contrast, and AI upscaling. The original is always kept, so every edit can be undone.
- Upload to Google Photos and OneDrive albums, or open a photo in your favourite editor.
- Light and dark mode with colour palettes; English, German, Spanish, Arabic and Chinese.

## Good to know
- The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- From now on the app looks for updates once a day. Settings › App › Updates picks Stable (releases like this one) or Experimental (test versions).
