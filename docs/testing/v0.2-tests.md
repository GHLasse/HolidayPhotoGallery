# v0.2: things to test

v0.2 is the editing release. This list covers every v0.2 story in the backlog and the other changes merged into
prep since v0.1.0 (PRs #110 to #194). Test on the latest experimental build from prep, on real trip photos as well
as the samples below. Write ✅, ❌ or a short note in the Result column, or tell me in the project thread.

All v0.2 stories are in prep, including **E-5** (Save as a copy, Make a copy, #195) and **E-12** (noise
reduction, #197).

## Before you start

Have these at hand; most rows say which one they need.

| Sample | Where to get it |
|---|---|
| A normal trip folder (JPEG and HEIC, some portraits, some landscapes) | Your own photos |
| Old flash photos with red eyes, and a pet photo with glowing eyes | Your scans or old camera folders |
| Night or indoor photos with visible grain | Your own photos (for E-12) |
| iPhone photo (Display P3, HDR) and a Pixel Ultra HDR JPEG | Your phone, or ask family |
| A photo edited in Lightroom (has Lightroom "develop" settings in it) | Any photo you once edited in Lightroom Classic |
| 360° test photos | `/mnt/project-files/samples/360/` in the project (see L-9 below) |
| A Google Takeout folder of Google Photos (with the `.json` files next to the photos) | takeout.google.com, Google Photos only, one small album is enough |
| A GPX/KML/FIT track or Google Maps Timeline export of a day with photos | Phone, watch or GPS logger |
| ExifTool or Windows Properties › Details, to look inside a file | ExifTool from exiftool.org, or right-click › Properties in File Explorer |

Tip: work on copies of your photos for the editing rows, so a bug can't touch the real ones.
